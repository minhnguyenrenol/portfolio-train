Six people tested it and scored it 81 on the System Usability Scale, against a target of 70.

In-flight product · design complete, with engineering

  • Problem. Donors don't trust that their money arrives, and crypto makes it worse: a normal blockchain wait looks like a failure. The client wanted one wallet for giving, sending and earning points, used by people with no crypto knowledge, on phone and desktop.
  • What I did. Designed the consumer app across 43 pages and built the design system behind it. Made one tracker for every money flow, redesigned the flows when the team agreed to move the wallet and identity gates to the end, and audited the donation flow against the spec. I was hired for the interface, brought the UX in one practice at a time, was promoted to lead, and split the work with a junior designer under rules I wrote.
  • Result. Complete, and built by the front-end team from the prototype; not launched yet. The baseline audit found 45 spec items met, 37 partly met and 16 missing, and I fixed the flow against the spec, line by line. At the end of my design work, six people tested the prototype and scored it 81 on the System Usability Scale, against a target of 70.
