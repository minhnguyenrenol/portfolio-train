After Phase 1, dropout fell by a relative 20%. In testing, applying took about 10 minutes instead of 21 to 25.

Phase 1 live · tested with 10 merchants

  • Problem. Small businesses that already banked with NAB had no simple digital way to start taking card payments. The application ran across seven mini-apps: 29 steps, 124 taps and 21–25 minutes. It asked for details the bank already held and pushed an account nobody asked for. 95% of the people who started it gave up.
  • What I did. Led the design across two channels and three products, and mentored Ami, a designer on the team. I agreed three measures with product before design, audited the application with analytics, a heuristic review and interviews, and helped leaders and 30+ stakeholders agree one group to fix first. Then I rebuilt that group's application as one app in four steps, and rewrote the referral form on nab.com.au.
  • Result. In testing with 10 merchants, applying took about 10 minutes instead of 21–25, with 50 taps instead of 124 and a SUS of 80.7 against a target of 70. After Phase 1 went live, dropout fell by 20% of its old rate. On the referral form, lead-to-sale rose 20% and submissions 12%, both relative to the old form. The work went to NAB's executive leadership and was featured twice in the bank's 2024 Product & Design Showcase.
