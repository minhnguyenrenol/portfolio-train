Five people scored it 83.5 out of 100 for ease of use, against a target of 70.

In-flight product · design complete, with engineering

  • Problem. A stablecoin is only worth what people believe backs it. Early designs for Unera's portal leaned on claims such as “fully backed” and “instant redemption”. Some were not strictly true, and none could be checked on screen.
  • What I did. Designed the web portal where people buy, hold and redeem hUSD, a US-dollar stablecoin. Cut the first release to four jobs, took every unprovable claim off the screen, and rebuilt Proof of Reserve so each number shows its date and its source. Designed the slow paths as states, and gave the public pages one motion rule: every moving piece shows money going somewhere.
  • Result. Complete and handed to engineering; not launched yet. Three claims came off the screen, nine jobs became four, and 259 functional and 95 responsive test cases were written next to the screens, so QA tests what was intended. At the end of my design work, five people tested the prototype and scored it 83.5 out of 100 for ease of use (SUS), against a target of 70.
