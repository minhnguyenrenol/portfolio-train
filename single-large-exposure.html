Live since Aug 2025 · tested with 5 bankers

  • Problem. Before every large corporate loan, a NAB banker has to check that the client's total borrowing stays under its limit: the single large exposure (SLE) check. Bankers did it by copying limits between a spreadsheet and the credit platform, then pasting the answer back into the submission. They had their own name for it: Slow, Long, Exhausting.
  • What I did. Led the design and coached Ariel, a second designer. I agreed an ExQual target and a click baseline before high-fidelity design. My first version copied the spreadsheet and tested “good, not great”, so I threw it away. Watching bankers again showed one spreadsheet doing two jobs, and I rebuilt the check as two tasks inside the platform.
  • Result. Five bankers rated it 4.57 out of 5 on ExQual, in the Platinum band, against a Silver target. Reviewing exposure takes 67% fewer clicks, and simulating a change 39% fewer. It went live in August 2025, and a month later the “establishing relationship” score reached 70 out of 100 for the first time.
