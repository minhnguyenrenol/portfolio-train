In-flight product · design complete, with engineering.

  • Problem. Operators needed one console to fix stuck transactions, manage community centres and run the points rules. In a money product, the tool that fixes a problem can also hide one, and the client ruled out refunds and balance edits from the console.
  • What I did. Made the overview a queue of what needs an operator now, put a reason on every privileged action, and made reward rules append-only. Wrote a states rule before the first module was built. Before the main review, I told the team which parts AI had drafted and asked them to cut what they didn't need.
  • Result. The team removed four features before anyone built them, and narrowed the first release to the Huma operators for the end-of-October date. The design is complete to spec v16, with 15 spec requirements and 11 review items traced to screens. At the end of my design work, five people tested the prototype and scored it 81 on the System Usability Scale. It has not launched yet.
